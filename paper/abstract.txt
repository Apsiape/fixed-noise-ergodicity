Can ongoing positive noise make the long-term behaviour of a finite local rule decidable? A natural noisy Wang rule is ergodic exactly when its raw tile set does not tile the plane, at every replacement rate up to 10^{-24} on Z^6 and 10^{-30} on Z^4. A related construction on Z^3 gives the same equivalence for north/east deterministic tile sets. On non-tiling inputs the lower-dimensional all-input nonconstancy probability on a finite window F is at most |F|C_Rexp(-t/24000), with an effectively findable input-dependent prefactor uniform in the rate; the exponent 1/4 is retained for rates up to 10^{-4535}. On those inputs their normalized space-time interactions have uniqueness among all Gibbs measures and exponential weak spatial mixing. Ergodicity, Gibbs uniqueness and exponential weak spatial mixing are therefore undecidable at one fixed rational rate. A different family on Z^6, of fixed range 11, makes uniqueness among translation-invariant stationary laws Pi^0_2-complete at the single rational rate 10^{-22}, under a stated published-source condition C_T; the corresponding translation-invariant Gibbs problem is Pi^0_2-complete on Z^7. The enlarged intervals are paper proofs with exact arithmetic. Lean covers only the version 2.2.0 intervals of the retained natural-rule cores; the machine-family core is unchanged. Finite forbidden patterns give forgetting bounds, while infinite tilings protect distinct stationary laws.
