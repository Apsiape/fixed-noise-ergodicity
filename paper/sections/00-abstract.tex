\begin{abstract}
A natural noisy Wang rule on $\Z^4$ is ergodic exactly when its finite raw tile set does not tile the plane. A cardinal checker and a fixed 512-of-768 quorum use the tiles themselves and one blank. A transverse-quorum lift of Kari's nilpotency reduction gives the same equivalence on $\Z^3$ for north/east deterministic tile sets. One explicit positive rational replacement rate, independent of the input, makes ergodicity undecidable in both families. For non-tiling inputs, amortized coherence and an all-path density lemma prove finite-window coupling from the past for all starts; tiling gives two invariant laws. A saturation theorem explains why the earlier budget-only estimate needs six dimensions. The retained $\Z^6$ natural rule has exponential all-input coalescence with a universal computable slope and uncomputable delay, all-Gibbs uniqueness and robustness. Its Theorems D and A are verified in Lean; the $\Z^4$ and $\Z^3$ results are not formalized. The inspector theory and $\Z^8$ machine-family Lean core are retained.\end{abstract}
